## The Bots — What's Actually There

Pionex supports 16 crypto trading bots, such as grid trading bots, trailing buy bots, trailing sell bots, DCA, and others, each with its unique functionality and strategy for monitoring market trends. Here's a quick tour of the main ones:

**Grid Trading Bot** — The workhorse. It works by placing buy and sell orders at set intervals within a predefined price range. The bot profits from market fluctuations without trying to predict the next big move. Bitcoin bouncing between $80K and $90K? The grid bot is basically printing small gains the whole time you're ignoring it.

**DCA Bot** — For the patient investor. You can set the DCA bot to buy a fixed amount of crypto daily, weekly, or monthly. Over time, this systematically builds a position, making it ideal for long-term investors or anyone looking to steadily grow their portfolio without constantly watching markets.

**Spot-Futures Arbitrage Bot** — The calm, low-drama earner. It buys in the spot market and shorts futures to lock in funding rate income, targeting 5%–50% annualized returns, and keeping it neutral by hedging, so you're not riding the market's ups and downs.

**Rebalancing Bot** — The portfolio manager you never hired. If you hold multiple cryptocurrencies, the bot adjusts your holdings based on predefined percentages. If your allocation drifts from 50% Bitcoin and 50% Ethereum due to price movements, the rebalancing bot will sell some of the outperforming asset and buy the underperforming one to restore balance.

**Leveraged Grid Bot** — For when you want more spice. It lets you apply leverage up to 5× to your grid strategy. Invest 100 USDT, and with 3× leverage, your bot is working with 300 USDT. That said, this one's best left alone until you understand what liquidation means personally.
